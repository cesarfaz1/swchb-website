How it works

From first call to cleared cargo in four steps.

  1. 01

    Set up your file

    Customs POA, importer ID (CBP 5106) and bond — we guide you through each form.

  2. 02

    Classify your products

    We review invoices and specs, assign HTS codes and confirm duty rates and preferences.

  3. 03

    File & clear

    Entries go to CBP and partner agencies; we track release and coordinate with your carrier or forwarder.

  4. 04

    Stay compliant

    Statements, post-summary corrections and records kept ready for any CBP request.
